# Billing policies

These are the currently confirmed operating rules.

- Last reviewed: 2026-08-29
- Transition authority: the approved monthly operating Sheet, checked against the website policy source.
- Effective month and policy source must be re-read at execution time. If the live source differs from this reference, place the invoice on `HELD` instead of using this file as authority.

## Billable and excluded states

- Regular monthly billing uses approved `미결제` rows.
- `휴원`, `퇴원`, and `이월` are excluded.
- `추가수강` is excluded from the regular monthly invoice run unless explicitly requested separately.
- Zero-won invoices are not issued or notified.
- A withdrawn student must also be inactive in Rallyz and the website for the effective period.

## Tuition and discounts

- One Sheet row represents one class; multi-class monthly tuition is the sum of billable rows.
- During transition, approved Sheet amounts control issuance but must be checked against website policy calculations.
- Do not apply sibling or multi-session discounts twice.
- Preserve Rallyz discounts separately with understandable reasons.
- Unexplained, partial-period, or stale recurring discounts require review.

## Shuttle

- Shuttle fee is monthly and must not be added repeatedly across class rows.
- Last-reviewed standard amounts: weekly once 10,000 won, twice 15,000 won, three times 20,000 won, daily class 20,000 won, unless the execution-time policy source confirms an approved override.
- Saturday and Sunday have no shuttle service.
- Boarding at the stop in front of Branch 1 is a free-shuttle zone.
- Explicit exemption overrides the normal fee.
- Pause/withdrawal rows do not contribute shuttle fees.
- Rallyz records shuttle as a separate `셔틀비` expense.

## Identity and anomaly rules

- Prefer student ID, birth date, phone, enrollment ID, and class over name-only matching.
- Preserve A/B suffixes for same-name students.
- Parent-name or phone differences require historical comparison and user confirmation.
- Hold instead of guessing on duplicate identity, conflicting status, missing class, impossible shuttle weekday, unknown discount, parent conflict, unexplained amount, or an existing invoice.
